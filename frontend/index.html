How a case runs Live
1 · Score the message Regex + keyword engine extracts UPI, phone, IFSC and bank account, then checks the registry.
2 · Send a counter-bait A “daily limit reached” receipt pushes the scammer toward a second, backup account.
3 · Export the dossier Transcript, identifiers and canary captures compile into a downloadable complaint PDF.
