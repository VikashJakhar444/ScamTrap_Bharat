        How it works

        Three steps, from a doubtful message to a filed complaint

        Nothing to install and no account to create. Each step below takes you straight to the part of the portal that does the work.

        01

        Check what you received

        Drop in whatever the scammer sent you. You get a score, the reasons behind it and every identifier that was pulled out of it.

        • Message
        • Screenshot
        • Voice note
        • UPI QR
        • UPI ID / phone
        02

        Keep them talking

        Hand the number over to the AI decoy. While it chats, every reply, account and phone number is captured with a timestamp.

        • Live chat
        • Automatic replies
        • Canary traps
        03

        File the complaint

        Everything is bundled into a 1930-format PDF with an integrity hash — ready to upload on cybercrime.gov.in or to read out on the helpline.

        • PDF dossier
        • Integrity hash
        • Helpline 1930
